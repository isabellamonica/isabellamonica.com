About

Born and raised in Northern California, Isabella Monica writes romance, upmarket fiction, literary fiction, and poetry. After many rigorous workshops, she graduated from the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa with a Bachelor of Arts in English before working as a copyeditor.

If you’re drawn to stories rooted in subliminal romance, desperate, visionary endings, and giggles over underscored societal themes, you’re in the right place.
